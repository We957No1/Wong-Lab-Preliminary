The OTF is then a weighted complex overlap integral: phase cancellation can reduce transfer even
when the supports overlap. Its modulus |T| is the modulation transfer function (MTF); its phase
describes phase shifts of intensity modulations. A cutoff alone does not tell us the contrast available
below it.
The difference-frequency interpretation follows directly from Eq. (4.4): two admitted field compo-
nents at f1, f2 produce an intensity term at q = f1 −f2. Each field frequency lies within fc, but
their separation can approach 2fc.
Keep distinct. The coherent cutoff fc is the cutoff of a linear field transfer function. It does
not assert that a coherent intensity image cannot contain a 2fc beat frequency. The incoherent
cutoff 2fc describes linear intensity transfer. Incoherent imaging can retain finer object-intensity
modulation than on-axis coherent illumination in a specified comparison, but it is not universally
better. The pupil has not enlarged, and neither have evanescent fields been recovered.
