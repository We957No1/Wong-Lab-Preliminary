2. Quartic/sixth-order expansion and least-RMS focus
  W = A rho^4 + B rho^6
  c00=A/3+B/4; c20=(A/2+9B/20)/sqrt(3)
  c40=(A/6+B/4)/sqrt(5); c60=B/(20sqrt(7))
  Add D rho^2 + P, with D=-A-9B/10 and P=A/6+B/5.
  Balanced RMS = sqrt[(A/6+B/4)^2/5 + B^2/2800].
  Here P is the added piston, not the original piston coefficient.
  Symbolic expansion, zero mean, zero defocus and RMS identities: PASS

4. A thin spherical phase-screen lens (not a finite-thickness singlet)
  Symmetric spherical thickness change: t(r)-t0=-2[R-sqrt(R^2-r^2)].
  Air phase delay: P(r)=(n-1)[t(r)-t0], f=R/[2(n-1)].
  Target-focus OPD: W=P(r)+sqrt(f^2+r^2)-f.
  Quartic coefficient: -(n-1)/(4R^3)-1/(8f^3).
  Sixth coefficient:   -(n-1)/(8R^5)+1/(16f^5).
  At n=1.5, f=R: W=-r^4/(4R^3)+0*r^6+O(r^8): PASS
  At R=50 mm, a=5 mm: quartic edge OPD A=-1.250000 um.
  c40=A/(6sqrt(5))=-0.093169499062 um after piston/defocus removal.
  This screen omits travel inside a thick lens; section 5 traces that travel explicitly.
